How Do I Use It?

It's easy. Just a few simple steps...

Click the new puzzle button . This will present a new puzzle with a random grade. (You can control the grades of puzzle you want from the New Puzzle tab on the options dialog.)

Alternatively, if you want to enter a puzzle from a newspaper or website, first enter the puzzle clues. Click on the clue button then enter the clue numbers by selecting the cell with the mouse (or cursor keys) and typing the desired number. To delete a clue, press the delete or backspace keys, or press the space bar. You can only enter and delete clues while the clue button is pressed. Depending where the puzzle is from, you may be able to copy the puzzle to the clipboard and then paste it using the paste button .

Enter "big" numbers. These are the numbers that form the solution to the puzzle. Click on the big number button , select the desired cell with the mouse or cursor keys, and type the number you want. To delete a number, press the delete or backspace keys, or the space bar, or, you can simply enter a replacement number. When the cell has a single pencil mark, you can double-click it to enter the number.

Enter "pencil-marks." These are the small numbers that show what numbers could go in the cell - also known as the cell's "candidates". Click on the pencil-mark button , then select a cell with the mouse or cursor keys and type the number. Alternatively, if the "big" number button is pressed, you can toggle a pencil-mark on and off by pressing the control key while you press the number key, and similarly, if the pencil mark button is pressed, you can enter a big number by holding the control key and pressing a number key.

You can also use the "number selector" to enter big numbers and pencil marks. See the number selector description for details of how to use it..

Note: some of the more advanced functions, auto-solving, auto-completion of naked singles, hints etc, require that the pencil marks are complete and correct. If you have manually modified the pencil marks, and then get into an impossible position, or get nonsense hints, or other strange behaviour, it may be worth either auto-calculating all pencil marks (), or even restarting the entire puzzle with auto-calculate pencil marks enabled (in the down position.)

See the description of the toolbar for other commands.
