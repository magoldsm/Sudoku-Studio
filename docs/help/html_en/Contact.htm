Registration and Contact Information

Registration
SadMan Software Sudoku is not free, but is shareware. Shareware is a concept that allows you to evaluate software before deciding whether to buy it. The license allows you to evaluate this software for a period of 30 days. After this period has elapsed, you must either purchase a registration or uninstall Sudoku.

SadMan Software Sudoku is priced at $14.95 (USD) or 9.95 (GBP) for a single-user license. You can purchase any of our products through either PayPal or SWREG, a software reseller. Please follow the appropriate link found on the Buy Now page.

Your registration key will be delivered via email. To enter your registration details, run Sudoku, click the Help -> Enter Registration Key menu option, and enter your name and key exactly as shown on your receipt. Once you've successfully entered your details the Enter Registration Key menu option is no longer visible, and the Help -> About box will show your details. Keep your registration details in a safe place, as you will need to re-enter them if you ever want to move the application to another computer.
